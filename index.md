<article class="cf-card" style="--track:#b94148">
<h3>GBA <span>Horizons</span></h3><p class="cf-zh" lang="zh-Hans">湾区潮见</p>
<p class="cf-label">Keynotes &amp; deep-dives</p>

<p lang="zh-Hans">面向高校教师及资深研究人员的访学讲座。承办方可在活动前至少七个工作日联系委员会，申请联合宣传。</p>

<article class="cf-card" style="--track:#a74306">
<h3>GBA <span>Dialogues</span></h3><p class="cf-zh" lang="zh-Hans">湾区对话</p>
<p class="cf-label">Cross-disciplinary panels</p>
<p>Committee-curated plenary panels within Pre-CHI and other pre-conference events. Researchers and industry practitioners discuss emerging technologies, regional collaboration and the development of the HCI community.</p>
<p lang="zh-Hans">由委员会策划的跨学科与产学研圆桌，纳入年度 Pre-CHI 及其他获批的会前活动。</p>
</article>
<article class="cf-card" style="--track:#007853">
<h3>GBA <span>Currents</span></h3><p class="cf-zh" lang="zh-Hans">湾区新潮</p>
<p class="cf-label">Lightning talks, posters &amp; mentoring</p>
<p>The annual GBA Pre-CHI brings together paper previews, poster sessions, doctoral mentoring and networking. GBA institutions can propose editions ahead of UIST, UbiComp or IEEE VR.</p>
<p lang="zh-Hans">年度旗舰活动 Pre-CHI 包括论文快闪、海报、博士生论坛及学术交流。其他会前活动由大湾区高校或研究团队申办。</p>
